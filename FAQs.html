Can I donate products offline visiting the NGO?
Yes, many NGOs accept offline donations. You can visit the NGO directly and inquire about their donation process.
They may have specific guidelines and requirements for accepting offline donations.
Can I donate second hand/used products
It depends on the policies of the NGO you're donating to. Some NGOs accept second-hand or used products, while others may only accept new items.
It's best to check with the NGO directly to see if they have any restrictions or preferences regarding the condition of donated items.

Why do we collect a tip amount ?
Sometimes prices of the products change during the course of the campaign, we use these tips to cover the hike in prices and deliver

Is crowdfunding legal in India?
Indeed, crowdfunding with an emphasis on internet donations for personal, medical, and societal purposes is allowed in India. All types of crowdfunding are deemed permissible in India by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), with the exception of equity crowdfunding. Certain crowdfunding companies are recognized as charitable trusts under the Income Tax Act. Under section 80G, contributions made to these donation platforms are deductible from income tax.
