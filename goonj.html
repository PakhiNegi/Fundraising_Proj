According to Oxfam report, over 46 million Indians were living in extreme poverty in 2021. Furthermore, the nation produces one million tonnes of textile waste annually, the disposal of which costs billions of rupees. At Goonj, we employ the underutilized urban waste to create a living through material processing and upcycling.
These materials, including shredded or see-through clothing, reels of cassettes, large or see-through clothing, etc., are also used to make products like aasans (sitting mats), cloth sanitary napkins, daris, rugs, and sujini (quilt/mattress), which, when distributed to rural areas, can alleviate material poverty.

Here are some key facts about this campaign:

  • Through Goonj’s collection camps, dropping, and processing centers, 9000+ tonnes of material get collected and processed annually.
  • Material that cannot be channelized directly is upcycled into cloth-based products. Through this, lakhs of man-days of employment are generated.
  • Additionally, ignored and marginalized communities become producers for themselves.
  • The products created and processed in these centers are channelized to rural areas as rewards and used as a catalyst to trigger large-scale development work in the most ignored pockets of India.

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to all our supporters and donors for their incredible generosity and compassion.
