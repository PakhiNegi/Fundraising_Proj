Kerala Flood Relief Fund

The Kerala floods of 2018 were one of the most devastating natural disasters in the history of the Indian state of Kerala. Here's a summary of the event including the amount donated, the destruction caused, and links to news articles related to it:

Millions of people were affected by flooding in various regions of the state due to excessive rains brought on by a confluence of variables such as the southwest monsoon and local weather patterns.

The Kerala floods of 2018 occurred during the monsoon season, primarily in the months of August and September. Heavy rainfall led to severe flooding and landslides across various parts of the state, affecting millions of people and causing widespread destruction of infrastructure, homes, and agricultural land. The floods resulted in over 400 deaths and displaced hundreds of thousands of people, making it one of the worst natural disasters in the history of Kerala. The state government, along with various national and international organizations, worked together to provide relief and aid to those affected by the devastation.

👉 A large humanitarian response was launched in reaction to the floods in Kerala, India, as well as worldwide.

👉 A large number of people, groups, and governments provided cash contributions, necessary goods, and volunteer labor to aid efforts.

👉 Contributions kept coming in long after the immediate crisis passed, bringing the total sum donated for Kerala flood relief operations to many crores.

👉 WhatsApp groups sprung up as Control Centers that coordinated help and support across various areas.
👉 A good majority of the population was involved in arranging supplies and help material in various ways, which helped the country in staying calm.

Destruction Caused:

👉 The floods resulted in the loss of hundreds of lives and displaced millions of people from their homes.

👉 Infrastructure such as roads, bridges, schools, and hospitals suffered extensive damage, hampering rescue and relief operations.

👉 Agricultural lands were submerged, leading to crop loss and affecting the livelihoods of farmers.

👉 The floods also triggered landslides in hilly areas, exacerbating the destruction and posing additional risks to residents.
